## 业务规则（实测验证）

1. 帖子按状态分 4 类：Active / Pending / Expired / Draft
2. 分类 Tab（All / Jobs / Property / Marketplace / Services / Community / Cars）与状态 Tab 可交叉筛选
3. 每页展示 10 条帖子，支持多页分页导航
4. 每条帖子卡片展示：缩略图、标题、价格、Exposure / Views / Favorites 曝光数据
5. Active 状态操作菜单：Edit / Share / Withdraw / Delete
6. Expired 状态操作菜单：Re-listing / Delete / Reason
7. Draft 状态操作菜单：Edit / Delete
8. Delete 二次确认弹窗标题："Heads Up"，内容："Once you delete the post, it will be deleted permanently. Do you want to continue?"
9. Withdraw 二次确认弹窗标题："Heads Up"，内容："Do you want to withdraw the listing?"
10. Share 操作复制帖子链接，页面底部弹出 Toast "Link Copied"
11. Expired > Reason 弹窗标题："Voluntary Removal"，内容："You have voluntarily taken down your post."，按钮 "I got it"
12. Re-listing 跳转至 `/publish/classified?id=xxx`，原帖子数据（图片/标题/价格）预填
13. EasyChat Settings 弹窗含 AI Auto-Reply 开关，开启后列表条目右上角出现 "EasyChat On" 标签
14. 点击帖子条目跳转至帖子详情页（ae.58v5.cn 域名），详情页含 Withdraw / Edit 按钮
15. Pending 状态为空时显示空状态图标 + 文案 "There's nothing here."
